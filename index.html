How I work

Find the friction.
Fix the structure.
Move forward.

Every business has its own operating logic. My starting point is to understand the people, structure, commercial model and obstacles behind the numbers.

From there, I help identify an appropriate ownership and corporate structure, connect the expertise a business needs, and find practical ways to simplify operations, strengthen foundations and unlock growth.

That may mean setting up a new venture, entering the UAE market, rethinking an existing group structure, or reshaping a business that has outgrown the way it was built.
